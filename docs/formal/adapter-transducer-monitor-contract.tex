\paragraph{Status.}
This is a proposed mathematical presentation of the accepted adapter-fidelity
design.  It is a review artifact, not a claim that the Python implementations
have been formally verified.  Section~\ref{sec:scope} records the maintainer's
accepted distinction between complete author-facing extraction and mandatory
exact projection during monitoring.

\section{Objects and boundaries}

Let $\Sigma$ be a pinned simulator producer and let $D$ be the explicitly
declared configuration and scene domain for one certification row.  A native
snapshot is $x\in X_{\Sigma,D}$.  A monitor observation is $o\in O$ and
contains a time/index pair, a finite entity map, optional spatial vectors,
scalar properties, component tags, and discovery metadata.

\begin{definition}[Manifest]
A semantic manifest $\mu$ is a finite family of field records.  Each record
states a native source accessor, producer provenance, owner/address
population, lifecycle predicate, value transformation, unit, coordinate
frame, exact or approximate relation, author-visible key, tags, and discovery
rule.
\end{definition}

The manifest defines the intended projection $\Pi_\mu:X_{\Sigma,D}\to O$.
It is therefore the semantic bridge between native state and the trace seen by
the monitor.  Simulator validity with respect to the physical world, and the
adequacy of an authored formula for a human intent, lie outside this bridge.

\section{Adapter as a stateful transducer}

\begin{definition}[Adapter transducer]
For a fixed manifest $\mu$, an adapter is modeled as the Mealy-style
transducer
\[
  \mathcal{A}_\mu=(Q,q_0,I,O\cup\Err,\delta,\lambda),
\]
where
\[
  I = \{\SpReset,\SpObserve\}\cup
      \{\SpScope(R):R\in\powerset(K_\mu)\}.
\]
Here $K_\mu$ is the finite author-key population declared by $\mu$,
$\delta:Q\times I\times X_{\Sigma,D}\to Q$ is the state transition, and
$\lambda:Q\times I\times X_{\Sigma,D}\to O\cup\Err$ is the output
function.  The state $Q$ may contain identity maps, prior time/index values,
tracking history, extraction scope, and backend caches; it need not be finite.
\end{definition}

This transducer model buys an explicit account of history-dependent behavior.
Reset, entity-ID reuse, dynamic populations, partial observations, scope
changes, and cache invalidation cannot be specified by a stateless function
$X\to O$.  The transducer alone is not the fidelity contract: an arbitrary
Mealy machine may emit an arbitrary trace.  Fidelity is the refinement
relation below plus trace-level obligations.

\section{Snapshot refinement}

For a native snapshot $x$, let $\mathsf{App}_\mu(x)$ be the manifest records
whose lifecycle predicates hold.  Let $\mathsf{Keys}(o)$ be the emitted
author-key addresses, including entity ownership.

\begin{definition}[Snapshot refinement]
An observation $o$ refines $x$ under $\mu$, written $o\preceq_\mu x$, iff:
\begin{align}
  \text{soundness:}\quad
  &\forall a\in\mathsf{Keys}(o),\;\exists! f\in\mathsf{App}_\mu(x)
    \text{ such that }a=\mathsf{addr}_f(x)
    \land o[a]\;\mathcal{R}_f\;\mathsf{transform}_f(x), \\
  \text{completeness:}\quad
  &\mathsf{Keys}(o)=
    \{\mathsf{addr}_f(x):f\in\mathsf{App}_\mu(x),
      \mathsf{disposition}(f)=\mathsf{included}\}, \\
  \text{metadata:}\quad
  &\forall a\in\mathsf{Keys}(o),\;
    \mathsf{metadata}_o(a)=\mathsf{metadata}_\mu(a).
\end{align}
For an exact record, $\mathcal{R}_f$ is equality after the declared conversion.
For an approximate record, $\mathcal{R}_f$ is the approximation relation
written in the manifest.  Unknown relations do not pass as approximations.
\end{definition}

The uniqueness requirement prevents two records from silently claiming the
same author address.  Completeness applies to the canonical unscoped
observation; lifecycle-inapplicable fields are not silently reclassified as
excluded.

\section{Inventory closure}

Every certification row names a finite native candidate denominator $F$ and
an enumeration procedure.  Investigation records form the disjoint partition
\[
  F = I_F\mathbin{\dot\cup}E_F\mathbin{\dot\cup}U_F,
\]
where candidates are included, categorically excluded, or unresolved.
Certification requires totality, disjointness, and $U_F=\varnothing$.
Absence from a search result, lack of demand from current formulas, and
unknown semantics are not exclusions.

\section{Trace refinement and failure behavior}

For native trace $\sigma=x_0x_1\ldots x_n$, let
$\mathcal{A}_\mu(\sigma)=o_0o_1\ldots o_n$.  Trace refinement requires
$o_t\preceq_\mu x_t$ at every observed step and all of the following:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item reset establishes a fresh episode identity namespace;
  \item timestep is nonnegative and strictly increasing after reset;
  \item observation time is finite and nondecreasing;
  \item an entity ID retains one class within the episode;
  \item vector dimensions, scalar domains, tracking state, and dynamic names
        satisfy the structural adapter contract at every appearance;
  \item declared discovery and runtime materialization agree with the manifest;
  \item an accepted but unsupported or unclassified native kind produces a
        contextual error rather than a plausible partial observation.
\end{enumerate}

Errors are therefore part of the transducer codomain.  For an out-of-domain
input, $\lambda(q,i,x)=\Err(e)$ is faithful when $e$ identifies the violated
boundary.  Silent omission is not faithful failure.

\section{Scope and criterion independence}
\label{sec:scope}

Let $\pi_R$ delete author fields outside requested key set $R$ while
preserving time, identity, surviving values, and semantics.  Discovery,
specification authoring, manifests, and completeness inventories use the
unscoped transducer $\mathcal{A}_{\mu,\top}$ and therefore expose every
applicable included field.  After specifications compile, monitoring supplies
the union $R$ of their required components.  Every certified adapter must then
satisfy
\[
  \mathcal{A}_{\mu,R}(h,x)
  =\pi_R(\mathcal{A}_{\mu,\top}(h,x)).
\]
This is an exact comparison over equivalent producer histories.  Scope is a
read, transfer, and materialization optimization only: it may delete
unrequested fields but cannot redefine surviving values, metadata, identity,
time, or lifecycle.  Ignoring an explicit monitoring scope is incomplete
implementation, not a second conforming mode.

For criterion object $c$, criterion independence requires
\[
  \mathcal{A}_{\mu,R}(h,x;c_1)
  =\mathcal{A}_{\mu,R}(h,x;c_2)
\]
for equivalent histories and arbitrary benchmark reward, success, verdict,
or authored-formula objects $c_1,c_2$.  A native criterion may be used by an
external oracle, but not as production observed state.

\section{Capability obligations}

Conditional profiles are selected by the accepted native domain and audited
surface, not by an adapter's self-selected flag.  Rigid body, articulation,
deformable state, deformable topology, contact, and physical sensor profiles
are mandatory when their candidate populations are applicable.  The same rule
applies to camera capture, rendering, scene geometry/poses, and automatic
wrapping: when a capability is present and usable in the accepted subject and
configuration, its profile is mandatory.  A conditional profile may be not
applicable only with source-audited, exhaustive evidence that the capability
is absent from that subject.  The adapter must not mutate the scene merely to
make an absent capability present.

Deformable state and topology are distinct.  Nodal positions and velocities
do not establish element connectivity, rest configuration, or verified
physics-to-render correspondence.

\section{Monitor composition}

Let $M_\varphi$ be the executable monitor for formula $\varphi$, and let
$\sem{\varphi}$ denote the reference STL semantics over an
observation trace.

\begin{theorem}
For a native trace $\sigma$ in the certified domain, if
\[
  \mathcal{A}_\mu(\sigma)=\Pi_\mu(\sigma)
  \quad\text{and}\quad
  M_\varphi(o)=\sem{\varphi}(o)
  \text{ for every supported }o,
\]
then
\[
  M_\varphi(\mathcal{A}_\mu(\sigma))
  =\sem{\varphi}(\Pi_\mu(\sigma)).
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Substitute $\Pi_\mu(\sigma)$ for $\mathcal{A}_\mu(\sigma)$ using adapter
refinement, then apply monitor correctness to that observation trace.
\end{proof}

This composition is the useful guarantee: the reported verdict is correct for
the manifest-defined projection of the pinned simulator trace.  It does not
guarantee that the simulator matches the physical world, that the formula
captures the research intent, or that an uncertified adapter row is faithful.

\section{Executable evidence versus proof}

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lll@{}}
\toprule
Layer & Required evidence & What it establishes \\
\midrule
Inventory & exhaustive classification and source pin & finite claimed surface \\
Manifest & machine-readable records and parity checks & declared field meanings \\
Adapter trace & enforcement, properties, differential oracle & measured refinement \\
Monitor & native/RTAMT/oracle comparison & STL evaluation correctness \\
Composition & both premises over the same trace domain & end-to-end trace verdict \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

An evidence label records how an obligation was checked; it is not itself a
proof.  A certification result must be derived from a closed inventory and
exact obligation coverage.  Callers cannot manufacture certification by
supplying a Boolean or a list of unverified ``pass'' labels.
